How this started

On a hot summer day in 2015, two Madison vegans crossed paths for the first time in the Hawthorne Library parking lot. They had come to protest against the now-infamous research puppy mill, Ridglan Farms, and they quickly discovered that they had another thing they were also both serious about: music.

Liminal Aberration is the mercurial duo project of Jeff Stanek on keyboards and Aaron Yarmel on strings. The commitment that first brought them together still runs through the work: pieces about the dogs who mean so much to us, and about the people who risk everything to secure their happiness, safety, and freedom.
